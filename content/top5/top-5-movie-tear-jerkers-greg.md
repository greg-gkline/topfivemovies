"Do my tears surprise you? Strong men also cry... *strong* men also cry."

-- *the Big Lebowski*

Despite the tough, manly exterior we all project, every one of us has one or two buttons that, when
pressed, somehow open up a tunnel into the pit of our being and expose some small kernel of emotion.
Oftentimes we are able to recover at the last possible moment, but sometimes, not. These are the films
that try men's souls...
