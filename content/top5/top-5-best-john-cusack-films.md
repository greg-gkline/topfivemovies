John Cusack is the hip version of Tom Hanks. Hanks plays the roles of
sensitive lovers (*Sleepless in Seattle*) and heroic World War II soldier (*Saving Private Ryan*),
Cusack plays the oddball lover (*High Fidelity*) and
the just-trying-not-to-get-killed World War II soldier
(*The Thin Red Line*). Nonetheless he is a gifted actor with a hotty
sister. Ok, I'm only kidding, I only find her mildly attractive.
