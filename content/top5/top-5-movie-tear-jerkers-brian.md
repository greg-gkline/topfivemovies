Probably the closest I've come to being driven to tears occurred during these movies. I want to clarify the scenes that were responsible. Of course, being a guy, I had to fight them off hard. Without doubt, *The Green Mile* was the toughest effort. Just about every woman in the movie theater was sobbing throughout the film. Boy was my bottom lip sore after that one.
