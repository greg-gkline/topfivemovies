Compiling a Top 5 Hitchcocks causes me great pain. The man simply makes too many good ones. My favorite of his films generally come from the 1950s but each decade contains its jewels. Here you'll find no shortage of innovative shot-making, classy acting, tasty plots, masterful scores, and good ol' fashion suspense.
