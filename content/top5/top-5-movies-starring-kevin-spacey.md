These were really hard to put in order, and you could argue pretty convincingly for any of the top four to be Number One. Ultimately, I ordered them primarily based on the size of Spacey's role in the movie, but bumped *The Usual Suspects* a little lower because it's not quite as good in repeated viewings as it was the first time. My guess is that *Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil* should also be in this list, but unfortunately I haven't seen that one yet.
