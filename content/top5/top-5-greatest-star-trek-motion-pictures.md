Should these be called anything else? It's not like they are motionless
pictures. Anyway, I had been thinking about this one for a while. Since TNN
has been showing all of the original cast Star Trek movies four times a week
for the past eight months and HBO has been showing the other three a great
deal, I'm familiar with all of them. The number one choice was easy. This
was a great film no matter what. A great hero, a great villian (played by
Ricardo Montalban without Tattoo) and one of the most talked-about endings in the science fiction world for years.
