Being a historian I take the accuracy of historical films seriously. To this day I can hardly watch Braveheart without shouting at the screen "The
Scotts didn't wear blue war paint!" and then be asked to leave the electronics section of Wal\*Mart. As far as my top fives go, you may notice Saving Private Ryan is missing. Saving Private Ryan is the greatest war
film and accurately depicts the storming of Normandy but the rest of the film was a fictitious story. So without further delay, my top five picks.
