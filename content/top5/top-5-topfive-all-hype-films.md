We have all been there. That annoying pest of a friend who nags all the time about having to see a certain movie. Finally you give him his day in the sun. You sit down with snacks and soda eager to view this film. You realize that the 2 hours could have been better spent elsewhere. This is in honor of those moments where a film was less than inspiring. I admit, I predominantly occupy this list... but it is unanimously accepted TopFive folklore that the all-time movie-pick blunder goes to Cuffy. I have included the guilty party for all to see.
