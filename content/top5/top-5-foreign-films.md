All of these are good films, even if they happen to be foreign, well,
technically, El Mariachi was filmed in Texas \[I think\], but it was in
Spanish so I say it counts... but I digress. I've comprised this list
of movies that could each be separated into their own categories.
Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon: Best Fight Scenes; Godzilla:
Cheesiest Fight Scenes; El Mariachi: Best Independent Film by a
Mexican-American for Under 7 Grand; and Zui Quan & Fei Lung Maang
Jeung: Best Fight Scenes/ Funniest Dubbed English in a Jackie Chan
Movie. Note: For all you illiterate, English-speakers out there who
aren't reading this right now, you may want to stray away from 3, because they are in subtitles.
