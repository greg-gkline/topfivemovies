Now before I start this, I know I'll suffer the slings and arrows of not
picking the obvious choice for number one by placing it second and the other
obvious number one by placing it third. In my opinion *Dog Day Afternoon* is
totally over looked as his best film. No, it wasn't an epic like *Godfather
Part II* or did Pacino play an eccentric military man like *Scent of a Woman*.
Instead Pacino played a common man who was put under extraordinary
circumstances.
