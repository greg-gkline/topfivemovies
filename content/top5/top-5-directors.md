This list was extremely difficult due to the vast number of high-caliber directors out there. It could very easily have been
a Top Ten list, but the name of this site is Top FIVE Movies, so I guess I've got to limit my choices.

That being said, I still
have to dole out a few honorable mentions to David Lynch, John Landis, Oliver Stone, and M. Night Shyamalan, who will probably
break into the Top 5 soon if his next few films display the same brilliance that the first couple have hinted at.
