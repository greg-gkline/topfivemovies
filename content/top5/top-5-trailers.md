I'm really dating myself with this one. I'm sure there have been
plenty of excellent trailers before these, but these are the ones that
I have seen that blew me (and many audiences, I'm sure) away. By the
way, does anyone know why they are called "trailers" even though they
come before the feature film? ...but I digress. Keep in mind, that
this list has nothing to do with how good the movie was, but how good
the trailer made it look.
