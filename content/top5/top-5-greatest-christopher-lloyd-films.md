Christopher Lloyd is a mainstay in American cinema. He's accomplished a
wide range of films, from comedy to westerns, film noir to science fiction.
He's an underrated actor and box office draw.

A bit of clarification on my list is probably needed. *Things to Do in
Denver when your Dead* was a film starring Andy Garcia, Christopher Walken
and Steve Buscemi, it's a great film. *The Lone Ranger*, although not one of
the most critically acclaimed films, was very good due to Christopher Lloyd
portraying a villain. Plus it's one of the first films I can remember my
parents taking me to see at the drive-in.
