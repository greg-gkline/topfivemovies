I've been less than quiet in my opinion about sequels, particularly this
summer's stinkers. The whole deal with sequels is to continue telling a
story about characters after one film ends. The reality of sequels is for
Hollywood to whore out good ideas to make more money. This is no more
evident than in these five movies that I hope no one saw other than me.
