Saturday Night Live has given us some memorable characters who've been
even greater on the silver screen. Jake and Elwood Blues, Wayne and Garth
and yes, even Leon Phelps. But there is a darker side. If you haven't seen
these, consider yourself lucky. Here are the five worst SNL character
movies of all time.
