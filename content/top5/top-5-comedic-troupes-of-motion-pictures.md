If getting two actors together in more than one funny movie is damn near
impossible (see [Top 5 Comedic Duos of the Motion Pictures]({{< relref "top5/top-5-comedic-duos-of-the-motion-pictures.md" >}})), then getting a bunch of
people ++is++ impossible. But it has been done with wonderful results.

Just a couple of notes, I know that not putting the Stooges first is
blasphemy, but the Marx Brothers utilized both physical comedy and dialog
better in my opinion. The Kids in the Hall are better known for their
televison show but they did make one film.
