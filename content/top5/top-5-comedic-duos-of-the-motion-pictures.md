Getting an actor to do a movie is easy, making a comedy is more difficult.

Getting two actors to be funny in one movie is possible, but getting the
same two actors to be funny in more than one movie is damn near impossible.
The following five comedic duo's all deserve to be number one, but in my
opinion Bud Abbott and Lou Costello perfected the funny guy/straight guy
routine that has shaped contemporary comedy. Many of you might wonder where
Lewis and Martin are on my list. I honestly never found Jerry Lewis, the
Pauly Shore of the 40's, very funny.
