Simply put into laymans terms, Jeff Goldblum has the divine ability to
use words in sentences that are made up of a complex series of innate
sounds. These sounds dictate to the listener, visions of extraordinary, or
should I say, elaborate concepts. Goldblum needs only to incorporate these
words, for a simpler term, into lines. These lines, if you will, are
projected from his psyche which is now embodied in a character that he
portrays in a film. Goldblum divides the superego and ego of the character
only to reunite both into the id of the audience who is now spellbound by
his incoherent ramblings both in his native tongue and in Esperanto.
Goldblum manipulates the contagion, begining with an "oooh" and "ah"
which turns into running and then screaming by any creature.
