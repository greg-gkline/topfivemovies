You know, it's really amazing what horrible forms of torture one will
subject themselves to when there is nothing else on the TV late in the
evening on a Sunday night. Such is how I was exposed to *Summer Catch*, a
schlocky, predictable, "feel-good" vomit-fest starring the worst actor of
our generation, Freddie Prinze Jr.

Summer Catch is the story of Ryan Dunne, a poor lawn-boy whose dream is to
play professional baseball. Dunne qualifies for the Cape Cod summer league,
a semi-exclusive league which doubles as a haven for being "discovered" by
Major League scouts. Early in the season, Dunne falls in love with Tenley
Parrish (Jessica Biel), the daughter of one of his rich lawn-care clients.

This is one baseball movie that has more cliches than Darryl Strawberry has
drug arrests. The girl encourages the guy to achieve his dreams. The rich
dad tells the girl she can't see the poor guy. The guy chokes in his big
game. Then, the guy gets a second chance...with both baseball and the girl.

I don't think I'm really spoiling anything to reveal that the cliches all
climax into the ultimate cliche: everything works out fine for everyone
involved and they all live happilly ever after. Whoopity-doo.

The only reasons that this movie got any points from me are for the
following reasons (one-point alotted for each): (1) The movie featured
baseball, the best damn sport in the world. (B) The movie featured Jessica
Biel, who although she possesses marginal (at best) acting skills, is still
totally hot. And, (3) the movie features a song from local Pittsburgh rock
favorites The Clarks -- a small excerpt during an early scene, and in its
entirety over the closing credits. I just wish for them their song would
have been used in a better movie.

So, if this movie is ever on late on a Sunday night when there is nothing
else on TV, go to bed, watch SportsCenter (it's always on), or carve out
your eyes with a dull spoon. Yes, even that is better for you than *Summer
Catch*.
