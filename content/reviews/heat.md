The DVD version of Heat contains just one special feature: a collection of three theatrical trailers. The third is okay, the first is better, but the second is awesome. Excellent action, superb score, and touches on all the best parts of the movie.

Like most pre-DVD era movies, it's not a big surprise to see no other special features, but it sure would be nice to have them. But that second trailer is pretty solid, and considering that the disc typically retails at around $15, it's a hell of a value for the price.
