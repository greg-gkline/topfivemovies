The year 1994 brought the world *Clerks*, Kevin Smith's first film. Critics
and movie-goers alike embraced two convenience store drug dealers named Jay
and Silent Bob. Now it's seven years later and the two pimped out Jedi
Knights have their own film.

Here's the story, Jay and his heterosexual life partner, Silent Bob, find
out that the comic book based on them is being turned into a movie. That
sounds good. Until they find out that punks on the internet were talking
trash about them. Nothing is more important that protecting your rep. So
the two go to Hollywood to wreck the film.

This is the movie I have been waiting for all summer. Did it live up to
its expectations? I think so. I am a die hard Kevin Smith fan and as I
understand it, this will be the last film with Jay and Silent Bob and the
entire Clerks Universe. They did lots of *Star Wars* jokes, spoofed other
classic films and brought back other Kevin Smith movie alumni like Jeff
Anderson and Joey Lauren Adams.

The problem with the film is that if you weren't a fanatical *Clerks* fan
you'd probably spend the entire time wondering why that one guy in the front
with the trench coat and Daredevil shirt is laughing so hard. A casual fan
wouldn't have gotten the Walt Flannagan's dog joke or understood why Holden
wished he was still dating a lesbian. Okay, some things are universal.

All in all, I liked the film. There was a lot of funny stuff and some
good spoofs. Charlie Average Movie-Go-Seer might not give *Jay & Silent Bob*
the high rating I did, but they probably feel bad for any independent
contracter who was working on the reconstructed Death Star in *Jedi*.
