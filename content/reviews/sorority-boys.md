*Sorority Boys* is somewhere in the middle of great college comedies. It
wasn't as funny as *Animal House* (1981), but much better than *PCU* (1994).
It is definitely an entertaining film and will be one of those great DVD
purchases.

Let me break it down for all of you who went to good colleges and have
trouble following plots. Barry Watson, Michael Rosenbaum and Harland
Williams are members of the KOK frat, the number one party frat in college.
They are also the head of the social committee. When the Booze and Cruise
money goes missing they are kicked out. In order to clear their names and
get good jobs they dress up like sorority sluts and sneak into a party. When
they are caught they are taken in by the Delta Omega Gamma a.k.a. DOG
sorority. Hilarity ensues as the three guys dress like girls to get back
into their frat.

There are many great parts to this film. Number one would be all
the scenes with boobs. Lots and lots of boobs. Second would be the fight
scene between Barry Watson and Michael Rosenbaum sword fighting with dildos
while dressed in drag. The third greatest part of the film would be the
fact they show lots of boobs.

Harland Williams was very funny and Melissa Sagemiller shows off her
boobs. There is also a lesbian scene but I'm not sure if it really counts
because it was between Melissa Sagemiller and Barry Watson who was dressed
as a chick. Still highly erotic because the illusion of two lesbians making
out is very strong.

In conclusion this was a great flick. Go see it with someone who likes
boobs.
