Top 5 Movies You Like But Don't Want to Admit
by Ryan
(July 19, 2001)
OK. Admit it. You've all been dragged to the theater or the video store by
your girlfriend to see another infamous chick flick. You settle in, hoping
to sneak a nap, when all of the sudden, out of the blue, a strange feminine
force seizes you. You are helpless as your eyes gravitate to the screen,
your brain begins to sort out a rudimentary plot, you even begin laughing at
jokes, and then it hits you, you actually like the movie. You don't tell
anyone at first, hoping it was just a bad dream, but then you see it again.
You laugh some more and cry some more and finally realize, you can't fight
it, it's gonna happen.
Little Women
- a sweeping romantic epic, irresistable.
Miss Congeniality
- A must-see starring America's Sweetheart and my
favorite actress, Sandra Bullock.
Wedding Planner
- J. Lo. Enough said.
Trial and Error
- A standard romantic comedy livened up by the antics of
Michael Richards.
Romy & Michele's High School Reunion
- I honestly have no real
justification for liking this movie, but I do.
Honorable Mention: Meet Joe Black
- It was a long strange night at TopFive. We wandered into the
movie store looking for an action flick. It was Saturday night and the
store was picked over pretty good. We panicked, lost our heads, and came
out with this.
