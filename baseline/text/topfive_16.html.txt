Top 5 Worst Films
by Matt
(June 29, 2001)
Here it is, folks... the worst films of all time. Of course this is
only my own personal opinion, but, let's be honest, what else matters?
I was swindled into seeing the first three films by my college buds
(Brian: The Thin Red Line and Lost Highway & Woods: Boxing Helena -
just for the record). Somehow I felt dummer, yes dummer, after
watching these pieces of cinematic crap. I was then later tricked into
seeing the cash-cow, Titanic, by my so called "friend" Dave out here in
Cali. (In his defense, however, he thought they would actually have a
historical telling of the ship, not a fictional account of three
passengers--go figure). To round out the list, Congo, a monkey movie
with virtually no plot, just didn't cut it as Michael Crichton's much
awaited book-to-movie project follow-up to Jurassic Park.
The Thin Red Line
Boxing Helena
Lost Highway
Titanic
Congo

To compare to ATRL I'd seen Saving Private Ryan. Nuff said.
