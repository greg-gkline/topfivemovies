Top 5 Funniest Sports Comedies
by Bruce
(October 04, 2001)
Buster Keaton played shortstop for his high school baseball team. One
would suspect that with Keaton's comic genius and his athletic ability he
would find these five movies dealing with sports to be the funniest. I
would agree. Just a side note, if you haven't seen
The Great White Hype
and
you're a fan of
Pulp Fiction
there's a subtle tie-in that I thought was
funny.
Caddyshack
( 1980 )
The Great White Hype
( 1996 )
Baseketball
( 1998 )
Major League
( 1989 )
Happy Gilmore
( 1996 )
