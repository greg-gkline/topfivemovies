Top 5 Greatest George Clooney Films
by Bruce
(December 19, 2001)
This week saw the opening of
Oceans' Eleven
, a remake of a Rat Pack film.
Who plays the part of Frank? George Clooney. How did he do? It's
debatable for two reasons. The first is that Frank Sinatra never played a
character in a movie, he just acted the way he wanted to and the character
acted around him. Clooney can pull this off sometimes. The second reason
it's debatable on whether or not Clooney did the Sinatra film justice, is that I haven't
seen it.
Clooney, who was best known for his role as Booker on the sit-com
Roseanne had done quite a bit afterwards. He starred in
Attack of the
Killer Tomatoes, part II
and played an undercover cop acting as a doctor on
the television series ER. At least that's what I've been told, I never saw that show
either. On with the top five.
O Brother, Where Art Thou
(2000)
Out of Sight
(1998)
Perfect Storm
(1998)
From Dusk Till Dawn
(1996)
Three Kings
(1999)
