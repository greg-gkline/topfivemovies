Top 5 War Movies
by Brian
(July 03, 2001)
There are many quality war movies out there. For many it is the realism factor that defines a good war movie. My top selection touched on the psychological aspect better than any, though. Many will debate this and I will address in a future article. Platoon gets on this list just because of the sad and beautiful Adagio for Strings alone. And Apocalypse Now is just flat twisted.
The Thin Red Line
Saving Private Ryan
The Dirty Dozen
Apocalypse Now
Platoon
