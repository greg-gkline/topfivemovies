The Royal Tenenbaums (2001)
Gene Hackman, Gwyneth Paltrow, Luke Wilson
Rating: 9.0 / 10.0
by Bruce
(July 13, 2002)
I had a professor in college who was eccentric. He would ride his bike
to the buildings year around and would reuse the same brown paper bag he
would carry his lunch in. One day I had lunch with him and noticed the
brown paper bag had dates on it. When I asked him about it, he told me they
were from the book he was writing. When I asked him when the book would be
out he laughed and told me he's been working on it for ten years and
probably wouldn't be finished for another ten. Okay, that might not be so
crazy, but if my professor was a character in the film
the Royal Tenenbaums
,
he would be the normal guy.
The Royal Tenenbaums
is a dark comedy centered around a family of
brilliant wackos. Gene Hackman plays Royal Tenenbaum, a low-life lawyer,
who is married to Angelica Huston, a complacent anthropologist. Together
they have three children, Ben Stiller, a financial guru, Gwyneth Paltrow, an
adopted playwrite, and Luke Wilson, a washed-up professional tennis player.
When the children are still very young yet accomplished in their own
rights, Gene Hackman separates from their mother. The children are comically
scarred in their own ways.
The cast that makes up the Tenenbaums and their cohorts is amazing. Bill
Murray, Danny Glover, and Owen Wilson are very funny in their subtle humor.
This film is not for everyone. This film is a very dark ride. The
story moves quickly as the children grow up and the wit is even quicker.
This is not your typical Zoolander-esque film one would expect from seeing
Stiller and Owen Wilson in the credits. The humor is very dry and highbrow
to say the least. If you were a fan of
Rushmore
( 1998 ) then I highly
suggest this film.
