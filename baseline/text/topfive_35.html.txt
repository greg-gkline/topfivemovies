Top 5 Most Shocking Movie Endings
by Greg
(July 24, 2001)
Undoubtedly there are many classic movies that probably deserve to be in this list. I'm sure films like
Psycho
,
The Empire Strikes Back
,
The Crying Game
, and
Soylent Green
stunned theater-goers at the time
of their release, but by the time I saw any of them, I already knew the ending. So unfortunately, most such flicks
were less shocking to me personally because their outcome has already become part of the American culture.
Having said that, the following are the five films that blew me farthest out of my seat:
Arlington Road
Unbreakable
The Sixth Sense
Se7en
The Usual Suspects
