Top 5 Worst Christmas Movies of All Time
by Bruce
(November 15, 2001)
Some of the worst films ever deal with Christmas. I think the evil
Hollywood Execs try and capitalize on the heartfelt sentiment of the season
and exploit it. Why can't they make bad holiday films around stupid
holidays like Arbor Day. The world may never know.
Just as a side note, I do belive that my number one was the worst film
ever in my opinion. Hulk Hogan + Ed Beagley Jr. + Santa foiling burglars
does not = Oscar.
Santa With Muscles
The Santa Clause
The Grinch Who Stole Christmas
I'll Be Home for Christmas
The Nightmare Before Christmas
