Top 5 John Goodman Films
by Bruce
(December 27, 2001)
John Goodman is not a leading man, but he's probably one of the greatest
co-stars in film history. His roles in film aren't always the largest, but
he plays them to the fullest. Goodman, by far, is the largest small role
actor in Hollywood.
Picking out five of Goodman's best films was tough. I stayed away from
the cartoons, for example,
Monsters Inc
. Even though Goodman was good in
them the cartoons don't do him justice. Goodman can say a great deal with
just his facial expressions. Far and away my favorite Goodman film is
The
Big Lebowski
(1998). Not only was it directed by the Coen Brothers, it
had one of my favorite actors in it, Steve Buscemi and it had one of my
favorite lines of all time in it spoken by Goodman: "You see Larry ! This
is what you get when you fuck a stranger in the ass!" Classic.
The Big Lebowski
( 1998 )
O Brother, Where Art Thou
( 2000 )
Arachnophobia
( 1990 )
One Night at McCool's
( 2001 )
Fallen
( 1998 )
