Top 5 Best Uses of Narration
by Greg
(June 20, 2001)
It may seem a bit juvenile, but sometimes there's just no better way
to tell a story than through a narrator or some character that talks
to the camera. This list actually turned out to be much more difficult
than I anticipated, because of the vast number of excellent movies that
use narration. By resolving to later revisit comedies in a category of
their own, the task became marginally easier.
So here are the best uses of narration in a non-comedy:
GoodFellas
the Shawshank Redemption
Fight Club
A Bronx Tale
American Beauty
