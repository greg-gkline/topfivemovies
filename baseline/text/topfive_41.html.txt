Top 5 Greatest Westerns of All Time
by Bruce
(September 02, 2001)
I'm a big western fan. I could never get into the Gene Autry singing
cowboy stuff however. The greatest westerns have to be the epic films,
films that make the west appear to be as big as it was in real life.
The Good, the Bad and the Ugly
(1966) For all of you Actors Studio Guild Fans a.k.a Buono, il Brutto, il Cattiuo
The Magnificent Seven
(1960 )
Hondo
(1953)
Tombstone
(1993)
How the West Was Won
(1962)
