About Us
Bruce
Contributing writer, honorary founding member
I grew up in the small town of Saegertown, PA which is just north of
Meadville. I hold a degree in Secondary Education Social Studies from
Youngstown State University. Currently I am a substitute teacher, which is
a better term for unemployed.
I have always been a fan of movies and a fan of putting things in order
from greatest to least. I also have the ability to count from the number
one to five in decending order. Greg once heard of this unique gift
and decided to test me on it. He gave me five blocks of Spam, all in
different order. Greg asked me to put the smallest first, then the next
largest and so on and so forth until I no longer had blocks of Spam. In two
hours I had finished his task. He was amazed, then asked me if I could do
the same with movies. I told him I could and a website was born.
