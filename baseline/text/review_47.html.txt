Suicide Kings (1997)
Christopher Walken, Sean Patrick Flannery and Jay Mohr
Rating: 9.0 / 10.0
by Bruce
(April 02, 2002)
Suicide Kings
was very similar to
Things to Do in Denver When You're
Dead
( 1997 ). Not only were both movies made in 1997 and starred
Christopher Walken; both films were dark comedies in the genre of film noir
and neither one got very much press when released. Both films also had a
large cast of notable actors who played small parts but played them very
well. Christopher Llyod and Steve Buscemi were both in
Things to Do in
Denver When You're Dead
while Dennis Leary and Johnny Galecki were both in
Suicide Kings
. I think it was the small role actors that made both films so
good. Both Leary and Lloyd added a dark humor to both films which offset
the bleak world of organized crime.
Suicide Kings is about Avery, played by Henry Thomas, whose sister gets
kidnapped by mobsters and ransomed for two milion dollars. In order to get
her back Avery and his friends played by Jay Mohr, Sean Patrick Flannery and
Jeremy Sisto, kidnap a notorious crime boss played by Christopher Walken to
get her back. The plan seems simple, but goes awry when the possibility of
someone setting up the group is detected by Walken. Can the group trust
Walken or can they trust only themselves?
The film is great. Walken is the second creepiest guy in Hollywood and
does a great job in this film. Leary is also funny, especially when he
takes the bucket from the homeless guy. The way the story is told keeps the
audience guessing throughout the film.
As far as the DVD goes, I was equally empressed. First of all it was
only $7.99 at Wal*Mart. Second of all it included alternate endings and
commentary by the director. It was a very good DVD all around.
