Top 5 Good Tom Hanks Films That Were Not Nominated for a Major Award
by Bruce
(September 16, 2001)
Tom Hanks is arguably the finest actor of our generation. This doesn't
need to be said by a humble movie watcher such as myself. Anyone could see
his collection of Oscars and Golden Globes on the mantle of his home if he'd
let you. Which he probably wouldn't.
(Just two side notes: I almost placed
A League of Their Own
as number one,
but then remembered Rosie O'Donnell was in that film.
Joe vs the Volcano
was made number one because Tom Hanks fought a volcano. 'Nuff said.
Secondly, "Bosom Buddies" should be mentioned, a great televison program about
cross-dressing that was over looked by the Emmys.)
Joe vs The Volcano
(1990)
A League of Their Own
(1992)
Punchline
(1988)
That Thing You Do
(1996)
Splash
(1984)
