Top 5 Greatest Christmas Movies of All Time
by Bruce
(November 15, 2001)
Christmas time is a special time for movies. These five are the greatest
and most heart warming of all time. I didn't put
It's A Wonderful Life
as
number one, which might be blasphemy to some, but Ernest did save
Christmas; Jimmy Stewart just saved a bank.
Ernest Saves Christmas
It's A Wonderful Life
National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation
Trapped in Paradise
Home Alone
Staff Responses
This list is a sham! You have blatantly disregarded such great Christmas movies as "Die Hard" and "Gremlins". Sure, those are violent movies, but they're heartwarmingly violent. This discrimination must be corrected at once. I did like "Trapped in Paradise" though. Dana Carvey is a stitch.
