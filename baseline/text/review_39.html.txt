Joe Dirt (2001)
David Spade, Dennis Miller and Brittany Daniel
Rating: 2.0 / 10.0
by Bruce
(January 01, 2002)
I bought this DVD for my mother for Christmas. She nearly disowned me.
Joe Dirt
is one of the worst films I have ever seen. This is "Hulk Hogan"
bad.
Joe Dirt
is how the United States would have been if the South
had won the Civil War: Guys in mullets driving crappy cars and
listening to Molly Hatchet.
David Spade plays Joe Dirt, a luckless and witless janitor in search of
his parents whom he lost at the Grand Canyon when he was 8. Dirt gets in
misadventures throughout the rural South trying to find his parents,
hilarity supposedly ensues, but never does. Instead of something funny, the
story jumps around; Dirt finds an asteroid which turns out to be a frozen
lump of shit, Dirt meets up with a Native American fireworks salesmen, Dirt
has sex with someone whom he believes is his sister. There's also a scene
that deals with Buffalo Bob from
Silence of the Lambs
, which only fizzles and
never comes though as being funny.
The cast was an all-star lineup that would have been great in any
other film together. Christopher Walken, who is creepy, yet did a good job
playing a janitor, Brittany Daniel, who is hot and did an excellent job of
playing a hot chick and even Kid Rock, who is white trash, did an amazing
job playing a dude who is white trash. If Spade would have played his old,
sarcastic character, instead of someone named Joe Dirt, the name says it
all, the film would have been great.
The special features on the DVD include a direct link to the website
(http://www.this_movie_sucks_my_white_ass.com) and outtakes and bloopers,
which aren't that different from the actual film.
This is by far, one of the worst films I have seen on DVD. The only
hope is that Spade becomes heavily involved in the art of voodoo and
resurrects the spirt of Chris Farley to make more movies with him.
