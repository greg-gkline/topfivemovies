The Mothman Prophecies (2002)
Richard Gere, Laura Linney, and Will Patton
Rating: 6.0 / 10.0
by Bruce
(July 03, 2002)
If this movie wasn't based on actual events that happened in West Virginia
it would totally suck. However, because some of things sort of happened, it
only sucks.
Richard Gere plays John Kline, a writer for the Washington Post. He and
his wife get into a car accident where she "sees" something, then later
dies. Gere is broken up about the whole thing, but life goes on. A year or
so later Gere is sent to interview the Governor of Virginia. He leaves his
house in Maryland and ends up in Bumfuck, West Virgina, totally confused on
how he got there. Horrific oddities ensue.
I actually identified with Gere in this film. I myself have gotten in my
car, ended up in West Virgina, and not known how. Oddly enough I was in
college all of those times, drunk and ended up at a strip club. No real
horrific oddities except the next morning I would always find a receipt in
my pocket for a massage and two hundred dollar tip.
The rest of the movie went downhill. Will Patton played a hillbilly
and tried to shoot Gere and Laura Linney played a cop who hooks up with Gere
at the end. This leads beautifuly into the sequel where Linney uncovers
shocking secerts about Gere's past in "The Gerbil Man Prophecies".
As far as the DVD goes, nothing great. Some behind the scenes stuff on
how they destroyed a bridge and a little about the true events. Everyone
has probably seen both on A&E a month before the movie came out. If you've
seen everything else, rent this, but Mothman isn't that great.
Staff Responses
Actually, I really liked this film. I thought it was pretty solid, and very creepy. And with scenes filmed in and around Pittsburgh, it gets bonus points. Not that that helped "Sudden Death", though...
