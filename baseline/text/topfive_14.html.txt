Top 5 Historically Based Films
by Matt
(June 29, 2001)
First off, is there any doubt that Steven Spielberg is the greatest
director of our time? But that's another top 5 category. Schindler's
List has to be the most powerful, and accurate, for that matter,
portrayal of an historical event I've ever seen on film. The thing
that separates it from the rest, is the amount of scenes that are just
plain hard to watch. I wouldn't wish some of the things in that movie
on my worst enemy, Woods. Saving Private Ryan is also very
accurate, it's biggest strength being the sense of patriotism it
imparts upon every American who finishes watching it. Another
Spielfilm as well as two biblical epics finish out this list of
true-life tales.
Schindler's List
Saving Private Ryan
Ben-Hur
Amistad
The 10 Commandments
