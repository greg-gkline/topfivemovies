Top 5 Greatest Stanley Kubrick Films
by Bruce
(November 15, 2001)
Kubrick is one of those directors who did not make quantity films but
quality films. All of Kubrick's films have a dark side to them, a quality
that is unmistakeable.
My number one and two films in my opinion have the greatest casts.
Slim Pickens, George C. Scott and of course the greatest actor with a
mustache, Peter Sellers.
Dr. Strangelove, or: How I Learned to Stop Worrying and Love the Bomb
(1964)
Lolita
(1962)
Full Metal Jacket
(1987)
A Clockwork Orange
(1971)
The Shining
(1980)
