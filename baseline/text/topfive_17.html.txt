Top 5 Sports Movies
by Ryan
(July 02, 2001)
For those of us who have been less than successful in the world of real
athletics, the movies offer the perfect refuge. For two hours we live
vicariously through the silver screen; overcoming obstacles, beating the
odds, and defeating formidable foes armed only with a Coke and popcorn. And
we take it all in with a quiet confidence, knowing that in the end, the good
guys will win, because its in the script. So for those who want to feel
that jolt of adrenaline and thrill of victory with no physical exertion,
here are my top five sports movies.
Hoosiers
Field of Dreams
Remember the Titans
Rudy
Rocky IV
