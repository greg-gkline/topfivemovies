Top 5 Worst Al Pacino Movies
by Bruce
(July 04, 2001)
For as good an actor as the man is, he's still come up with quite a few
stinkers. These films weren't his fault, but he's still guilty by
association.
Dick Tracy
Heat
Frankie and Johnny
City Hall
Devil's Advocate
Staff Responses
Once again, I must disagree. "And Justice for All" and "Sea of Love" are hands down the worst Pacino movies...
While Heat has its flaws, it certainly is not one of his worst movies. The dynamic exchanges between he and Robert DeNiro alone make it worth seeing. Also, it has one of the best gunfight scenes in cinema history.
