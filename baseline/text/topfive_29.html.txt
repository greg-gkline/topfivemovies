Top 5 Cameos by a Musician
by Matt
(July 17, 2001)
Many times artists in the musical arena will see if they can replicate
their success on the silver screen. That being said, most of the time,
they play themselves, and it's a less than starring role. Regardless,
here are some of the best cameos by musicians that I've seen.
Alice Cooper
Wayne's World
-as himself
Flea
Back to the Future II and III
-as Douglas J. Needles
James Brown
The Blues Brothers
-as the Reverend Cleophus James
KISS
Detroit Rock City
-Gene Simmons, Paul Stanley, Ace Frehley, and Peter Criss: as
themselves
Ozzy Osbourne
Little Nicky
-as himself
