Top 5 Greatest Stanley Kubrick Films

by Bruce
(November 15, 2001)

Kubrick is one of those directors who did not make quantity films but quality films. All of Kubrick's films have a dark side to them, a quality that is unmistakeable.

My number one and two films in my opinion have the greatest casts. Slim Pickens, George C. Scott and of course the greatest actor with a mustache, Peter Sellers.

  1. Dr. Strangelove, or: How I Learned to Stop Worrying and Love the Bomb
    (1964)
  2. Lolita
    (1962)
  3. Full Metal Jacket
    (1987)
  4. A Clockwork Orange
    (1971)
  5. The Shining
    (1980)
