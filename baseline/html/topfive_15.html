Top 5 Foreign Films

by Matt
(June 29, 2001)

All of these are good films, even if they happen to be foreign, well, technically, El Mariachi was filmed in Texas [I think], but it was in Spanish so I say it counts... but I digress. I've comprised this list of movies that could each be separated into their own categories. Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon: Best Fight Scenes; Godzilla: Cheesiest Fight Scenes; El Mariachi: Best Independent Film by a Mexican-American for Under 7 Grand; and Zui Quan & Fei Lung Maang Jeung: Best Fight Scenes/ Funniest Dubbed English in a Jackie Chan Movie. Note: For all you illiterate, English-speakers out there who aren't reading this right now, you may want to stray away from 3, because they are in subtitles.

  1. Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon
  2. Godzilla
  3. El Mariachi
  4. Zui Quan
  5. Fei Lung Maang Jeung
