Top 5 Finest Samuel L. Jackson Performances

by Bruce
(October 25, 2001)

Samuel L. Jackson is arguably the greatest African American actor of all time. In every film Jackson is in, he always portrays a persona that is unique. In laymen's terms, he's cool. In all of his films he's cool, dare I say, cooler than Fonzie.

This, by far, is the most difficult top five I've ever done. It's impossible to limit Jackson's work to five best. I'm sure the film world will call me a heretic by my rankings and leaving off the films A Time to Kill (1996) and Eve's Bayou (1997) but in my defense I've never seen them. My number one film, Jackie Brown, is number one because it was Jackson who carried the film. Otherwise Jackie Brown would have been a total disaster in my opinion. Other honorable mentions would be Unbreakable (2000) and The Great White Hype (1997).

  1. Jackie Brown
  2. Shaft
  3. Pulp Fiction
  4. The Negotiator
  5. The Long Kiss Goodnight
