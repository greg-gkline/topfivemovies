Top 5 Worst Sequels of All Time

by Bruce
(August 26, 2001)

I've been less than quiet in my opinion about sequels, particularly this summer's stinkers. The whole deal with sequels is to continue telling a story about characters after one film ends. The reality of sequels is for Hollywood to whore out good ideas to make more money. This is no more evident than in these five movies that I hope no one saw other than me.

  1. Superman IV
    (1987)
  2. Caddy Shack 2
    (1988)
  3. Meatballs 2
    (1984)
  4. Problem Child 3
    (1995)
  5. Police Academy: Mission to Moscow
    (1994)

Staff Responses

Yes, Supe 4 was bad, way too preachy. That's what you get for letting Christopher Reeve write the screenplay. But how could you forget "From Dusk Til Dawn 2", or "Blues Brothers 2000"? The latter not only sucked balls, but also diminished the public memory of the original. Plus it had NO Belushis in it. What a rip.
